Questions

Before you sign up.

Is the Mac app still free?

Yes. Kill Cord for Mac stays free, with no account, and keeps your keys in your Keychain. Teams is a separate, paid service for companies.

When does Teams launch?

There’s no date yet. We’ll open it to a small group of companies first, starting with the waitlist.

How does it know who spent what?

From the providers’ own billing data. Anthropic and OpenAI report usage by API key, and Teams matches each key to the person who owns it, with no proxy and without seeing your requests. Other providers show up in the company total.

What happens to my email?

It goes to our email list (run on Kit) with your answers, tagged for Teams. We use it to tell you when Teams opens. Unsubscribe from any email. Privacy details.
