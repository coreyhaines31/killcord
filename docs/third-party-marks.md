# Third-party marks

Kill Cord shows a provider's logo only to identify that provider's service. Provider names and logos are trademarks of their owners, used to identify the services. Kill Cord is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

Rules for adding a mark:

- Use only the vendor's official brand or press asset, the monochrome (one-colour) variant, as vector.
- Commit the file unmodified. It lives in `KillCord/Assets.xcassets/ProviderMarks/mark-<provider>.imageset` as a template image, so it renders in the panel's label ink (#161616 light, #E6E7E8 dark), which matches the vendor's black or white mono variant.
- Never recolour beyond that, distort, crop, or add effects. Keep clear space: the mark fills about half of its key.
- If the vendor publishes no mono vector, or its guidelines need written permission for this use, keep the letter monogram and record why below.
